WWII PATROL

1940s attack submarine. Hunt convoys in the open ocean and raid coastal anchorages. You run on diesels on the surface and on batteries submerged. Torpedoes run straight, so you have to work out a firing solution.

  • Close in fast on the surface, then dive to periscope depth for the attack.
  • Every time you raise the periscope you risk being spotted. Aircraft see a surfaced boat from kilometres away.
  • Destroyers hunt with ASDIC. Go slow, go deep below the thermal layer (90 m), and release decoys.
  • Watching a target through the periscope improves the firing solution. A zig-zag throws it off again.
